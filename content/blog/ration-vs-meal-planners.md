**Ration is a live kitchen system for web and iOS: food stock (Cargo), Meals (Galley), weekly meal plans (Manifest), shopping lists (Supply), multiple kitchens, and private Daily Fuel macros from the Meals you cook. It also runs an MCP server, so AI assistants like Claude and ChatGPT can work with your real kitchen data.** Cooking a Meal takes its Ingredients out of your stock, and your Supply List is what the plan needs minus what you already have. This post shows where those influences come from, how Ration compares with well-known apps in each type, and how to choose which parts of Ration to use. You don't have to use all of them.

## Where Ration's ideas come from

### Cargo: your stock, from pantry apps

Pantry apps such as Paprika's pantry and Eat This Much's virtual pantry showed that knowing what you have changes what you buy and cook. Ration's Cargo goes a step further. You can add Items by taking a photo and letting AI read them in, or tell Copilot, Ration's built-in assistant, what you bought in plain words. When you cook a Meal, Cargo updates by itself.

**Lean into Cargo if** food goes off before you use it, or you keep buying things you already have.

### Galley: your Meals, from recipe managers

Recipe managers like Paprika and Plan to Eat proved people want their own library, not just someone else's catalogue. In Ration's Galley you can import a Meal from a website, or have AI analyse a TikTok video and pull out the Ingredients and steps. You can also ask AI to generate a Meal from what you have.

**Lean into Galley if** your recipes are scattered across bookmarks, screenshots and saved videos.

### Manifest: your week and your macros, from planners and trackers

Meal planners like Mealime and Plan to Eat organise the week, and Eat This Much showed a plan can double as a macro tracker. Ration's Manifest lets you plan Meals across the week and track macros from what you actually cook, so there's no second food diary. On iOS, a dedicated macro analytics page shows how you're tracking over time.

**Lean into Manifest if** you want to hit protein or calorie goals without logging every meal by hand.

### Supply: your lists, from shopping list apps

Shared list apps like AnyList set the standard for the shopping trip itself. Ration's Supply builds your Supply List from what your plan needs minus what's already in Cargo. You can now keep multiple Supply Lists, for example one for the weekly shop and one for a party or a different store.

**Lean into Supply if** the shop is where your week falls apart.

## How to choose

- **If you only want a recipe library**, Paprika 3 or Plan to Eat are strong, focused choices.
- **If shopping with someone else is the main problem**, AnyList is built for that.
- **If you want dinners chosen for you**, Mealime does it well.
- **If calorie and macro targets drive everything**, Eat This Much plans to the numbers.
- **If you want stock, Meals, the plan, macros and lists to update each other**, that's what Ration is for.

You don't have to switch everything at once. Many people start with one section of Ration, such as Supply or Cargo, and add the others when they're useful.

## FAQ

**What is Ration?**

Ration is a live kitchen system for web and iOS covering food stock (Cargo), Meals (Galley), weekly meal plans (Manifest), shopping lists (Supply), multiple kitchens and private Daily Fuel macros from the Meals you cook. Cooking updates your stock, and your shopping list is built from what's missing.

**How is Ration different from Paprika?**

Paprika is a strong recipe manager with planning, lists and a pantry. Ration's difference is that cooking a Meal automatically takes its Ingredients out of your stock, your shopping list is built from the plan minus your stock, and macros come from the Meals you cook. It also adds AI tools such as photo import and Copilot.

**Can Ration import recipes from TikTok?**

Yes. Ration can analyse a TikTok video with AI and turn it into a Meal with Ingredients and steps. It also imports from websites.

**Does Ration track macros?**

Yes. Ration tracks macros from the Meals you plan and cook, and the iOS app has a dedicated macro analytics page. It isn't medical advice.

**Can I have more than one shopping list in Ration?**

Yes. Ration supports multiple Supply Lists.

**Do I have to use every part of Ration?**

No. Each section works on its own, and they get more useful when they're connected.
